# Training Evaluation Form — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and go.

**What this is:** The Kirkpatrick-ready evaluation pack for corporate training: 8 reaction items, 3 confidence self-reports, keep/change open questions, plus the day-30 follow-up that turns feedback into behavior evidence — and a one-page report a manager can forward as-is. any group size · 7 min on the day + 3 min at day 30.
