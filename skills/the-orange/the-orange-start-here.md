# The Orange — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and go.

**What this is:** Two teams, one orange, one hidden fact each. Position-takers split it in half; interest-askers both walk away whole. Confidential brief cards and a pre/post measure included. 2 teams of 2–4 · 20 min.
