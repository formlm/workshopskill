# Draw What I Mean — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and run.

**What this is:** A 12-minute, pairs tool that puts three rounds of describe-and-draw side by side — free words, a shared shape-word card, then open questions — to show that instruction precision and a shared shorthand beat a confident description. Printable cards, a run sheet, no account. pairs (2 people) · 12 min · offline + online.

## Pick your lane

- **Just run it (5 min):** print a picture card per pair and a sketch sheet, run the three rounds, compare, bridge. No projector, no account.
- **Facilitate a bigger room:** pairs work simultaneously; you run the clock and the bridge from the wall poster.
- **Make the vocabulary yours:** swap the generic shape words on the card for your domain's real terms — the round-2 effect is the point.
