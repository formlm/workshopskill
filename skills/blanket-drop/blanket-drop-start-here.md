# Blanket Drop — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and go.

**What this is:** A 10-minute, 8–20 person name-game icebreaker where the blanket drops and the first team to shout the right name wins the round — every name in the room gets learned at a speed introductions cannot match. A spoken safety rule and a pre/post measure included. 8–20 people (two teams) · 10 min.
