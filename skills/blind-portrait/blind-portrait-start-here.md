# Blind Portrait — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and go.

**What this is:** Back-to-back blind drawing: one describes, one draws, and the debrief quantifies both communication styles. Prop cards, timing card and a pre/post measure included. 2 people · 15 min.
