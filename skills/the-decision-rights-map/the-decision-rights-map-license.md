# The Decision Rights Map — license & tiers

Plain-language summary of what you can do with this folder. The legal text lives at creativecommons.org; this page says it in human words — plus two paid tiers for people who need more than CC BY.

Everything in Tier 0 is free forever and never becomes paid just because you bought something else. The cards, the poster, the participant workbook, the one-page session sheet, the slide deck and the run sheet all sit in Tier 0.

## Tier 0 — free (CC BY 4.0)

Run it, copy it, translate it, remix it, hand it to your team, use it in a paid workshop. Two strings only:

- Keep the attribution line: "The Decision Rights Map · WorkshopSkill · CC BY 4.0."
- Do not imply we endorse you or ran your session.

No account, no email wall, no checkout form anywhere in this folder.

## Tier 1 — one event or one product (paid)

For when CC BY's attribution requirement is a hard blocker — e.g. client legal won't allow a third-party credit on delivered material, or you sell it white-labelled on a marketplace.

- Waives attribution for **one** event or **one** listed product.
- Includes print-ready masters (cards at poker spec, poster at A3) plus a white-label swap pass.
- One folder, one purchase. Not a license to redistribute the source itself.

## Tier 2 — organization (paid)

For a team putting this folder into an internal library, LMS, or facilitator handbook used across many people and sessions.

- Waives attribution for internal organizational use.
- Includes editable print masters and a named contact for version updates.
- Does not cover reselling the folder as your own product.

## How to request a tier

Email `mailto:hello@workshopskill.com` with the folder name (`the-decision-rights-map`), the event or organization, and which tier you want. This page deliberately has no checkout form — everything here runs offline, no account, no tracking, answered by a person.
