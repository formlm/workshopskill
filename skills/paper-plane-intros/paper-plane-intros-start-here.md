# Paper Plane Intros — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and run.

**What this is:** A 15-minute, 8–20 person icebreaker where everyone writes one non-obvious line about themselves, folds it into a paper plane, throws it across the room, then hunts down the author and asks a real follow-up question. The throw forces the room past the people it already sat next to — two rounds plus a gallery. Printable instruction cards, no account. 8–20 people · 15 min · offline + online.

## Pick your lane

- **Just run it (5 min):** hand out a plain sheet per person, show the two rules, fold and throw, read-and-find, then a quick gallery. You need open floor and a little noise.
- **Run it remote:** everyone "throws" by dropping their line into a shared channel; you read one back and the group finds the author. The paper version works identically on a screen.
- **Make the asks yours:** the instruction card lists example "non-obvious" prompts — swap them for your group's real ice.

## Print list (per person)

One plain A4 sheet per person to write and fold (any paper works), plus one instruction card and one follow-up card for the room. The `paper-plane-intros-activity-poster.pdf` goes on the wall so the two rules stay visible while you facilitate. Nothing needs a projector.
