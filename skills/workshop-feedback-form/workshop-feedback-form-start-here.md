# Workshop Feedback Form — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and go.

**What this is:** The exit ticket for public workshops: 4 rating items, one 0-10 recommendation score, two open questions. Ninety seconds at the door — and the feedback turns into two assets: permissioned testimonials for your workshop page, and a voted topic list for your next quarter. any group size · 90 seconds to complete.
