# Two Truths and a Lie — Start Here

> One page, thirty seconds, then print and go.

**What this is:** A 15-minute, 8–20 person icebreaker that rebuilds the classic getting-to-know-you game with two guardrails — public work facts only, eyes-closed voting — so everyone leaves with three checkable facts about every colleague. A spoken boundary rule and a pre/post measure included. 8–20 people · 15 min.
